Welcome to the official portal for the JWJ Marine Sciences and Astronomy Club. Founded by Yuvan Rajpurkar and Smaran Suresh in 2025.

About

In the JWJ Marine Science and Astronomy Club, we empower students with knowledge across marine sciences, oceanography, astronomy, and environmental conservation through high-level research and hands-on participation.

Competitions

We actively participate in NOAA's JPSS Virtual Science Fairs, MATE ROV underwater robotics, and the International Astronomy and Astrophysics Competition (IAAC).

Projects

Current initiatives include building 3D-printed Hadley telescopes, developing low-cost Arduino shark monitoring tags, and contributing data to the NASA GLOBE North America Phenology Campaign.
