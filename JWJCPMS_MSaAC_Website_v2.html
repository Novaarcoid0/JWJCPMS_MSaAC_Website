Welcome to the JWJ Marine Sciences and Astronomy Club

Founded by Yuvan Rajpurkar and Smaran Suresh in 2025. Explore the ocean depths and the cosmos with us.

Who we are

About Our Club

In the JWJ Marine Science and Astronomy Club, we empower students with knowledge across marine sciences, oceanography, astronomy, and environmental conservation through high-level research and hands-on participation. We will also do our research analysis in the Kangaroo Lab in JWJCPMS. If you have any questions, feel free to email yrajpurkar11@gmail.com.

Where we compete

Competitions

We have participated in NOAA's JPSS Virtual Science Fairs in the 2025-2026 school year, we will participate in the MATE ROV underwater robotics competition in the 2026-2027 school year, and we have had students who participated in the International Astronomy and Astrophysics Competition (IAAC) in the 2025-2026 school year.

What we're building

Projects

In the first year, 2025-2026, we built a 3D-printed Hadley telescope with NEFAS. In the 2026-2027 school year, we will do several research projects with the City of Jacksonville, Florida and we will test tools that the robotics teams in James Weldon Johnson College Preparatory Middle School have built for their innovation projects. We have also developed a low-cost Arduino shark monitoring tag called the Thresher Shark Tag, and contributing and contributed data to the NASA GLOBE North America Phenology Campaign.

We will be using our telescopes for the first time in the 2026 International Observe the Moon Night with NEFAS. It will be at Hanna Park from 6:40 pm to 9pm. The address is 500 Wonderwood Drive, Jacksonville, FL 32233 and we will meet in Parking lot 8. The entrance fee is $5 and if you come, please ask for a map so you know where we are.
